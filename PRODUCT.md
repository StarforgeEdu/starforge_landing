## Users

- Primary landing-page audience (inferred from the product suite): owners, directors, and managers of education centers who are evaluating a unified operating system for their organization.
- Product users confirmed by the existing applications: directors, department heads, teachers, teaching assistants, media staff, reception and admissions staff, print operators, cashiers, and other permissioned education-center employees.

## Product Purpose

Starforge is a multi-tenant education-center platform that brings leadership, academics, people, finance, operations, communication, content, reporting, staff workflows, and assessment tools into one permission-aware system across web, mobile, and desktop products.

The confirmed brand promise is: “Technology built for the people shaping tomorrow.”

For the landing page, success means helping a qualified education-center decision-maker understand the system, compare the three supplied plans, and start a direct conversation through Telegram, phone, email, or Instagram.

## Positioning

Starforge connects CEO web and mobile apps, staff web and mobile apps, and a mobile-only family app to the same education-center ecosystem. The existing source and user-confirmed product scope support a truthful position around one connected operational system rather than a collection of disconnected school tools.

## Operating Context

- Education centers may have multiple branches, departments, roles, and dynamically scoped permissions.
- Leadership uses CEO web and mobile apps for organization-wide and branch-scoped decisions.
- Staff use web and mobile workspaces for teaching, people, tasks, messaging, content, printing, finance, and operational workflows as permitted.
- Families use a dedicated mobile app; there is no family web product.
- Desktop IELTS preparation tools are in progress. A permissioned teacher or team member can upload or choose a mock exam, schedule it, generate a random room code, and have students enter that code in a desktop Mock Room to open the assigned test. The landing page must label this workflow as coming soon until availability is confirmed.
- The product is Uzbek-default and supports Uzbek, Russian, and English in the existing staff products.
- Prospective customers are expected to contact Starforge directly; no self-serve signup or checkout URL was provided.
